01  /  Understanding

Not a golf app. A summer movement layer.

On the North Coast, the private car dies at the compound gate. From there, people live in golf cars — villa to beach club, souk to marina, kids to the commercial strip. Golfi books that ride. The guest is a vacationer, not a player.

Golfi asked for a mobile product that lets guests find a golf car in their area, book it, pay, and ride — with a web dashboard so operations can run the fleet, and a guest web app so a villa can book from a browser without installing anything. The question underneath was commercial: why would that cost $20,000, $30,000, or $55,000?

The honest answer is that all three build the same list of features. What changes is how deep each one goes. Authentication can be a password and an SMS code, or it can verify that this phone really belongs to that villa. A payment gateway can hand a failed card to your customer service, or it can retry, refund and reconcile on its own. Booking can hold a slot, or it can price Friday differently and reassign a car that breaks down. Same feature, three depths, three prices — and section 03 lets you set each one and watch the bill.

Quiet

Cream, bronze, champagne. A product that looks like the villa, not a ride-hailing template.

Honest

A slot that is booked is gone. Guest and operator see the same car, the same hour.

Local

Sahel hours, compound gates, beach destinations — not a generic “golf course rental.”

Owned

Source, stores, and data belong to Golfi. NixAI builds and hands over.

    06  /  Architecture

    The stack you asked for.

    React Native, Node.js, MongoDB, Next.js — one team ships iOS, Android, and admin against a single API, then adds a compound without rewriting the summer.

    Mobile — React Native

    • One codebase for iOS and Android, native store binaries
    • Home, cars, profile, booking, pay, my rides, account
    • Gateway checkout in the booking, not a detour to a browser
    • Push, offline cache and deep links from Standard depth upward

    API — Node.js + MongoDB

    • REST for app and admin
    • Collections: users, cars, compounds, slots, payments, notifications
    • Slot reservation with conflict checks — no double book on Friday
    • Indexes for area and time as the coast catalog grows

    Admin — Next.js

    • Server-rendered dashboard, role-aware routes
    • CRUD for cars, compounds, hours, and the book
    • Payment ledger; analytics at whichever level you select
    • Audit of who moved a ride or changed a rate

    Brand, hosting & ownership

    • Cream, bronze, champagne tokens — no generic teal rental kit
    • Staging and production under your accounts
    • Repositories, keys, and store listings in Golfi control
    • 30 days of hypercare after store approval
