01 / Executive Summary

A flagship event on a template-grade website.

Around 45 international speakers, hands-on workshops, a pre-congress cadaver lab, 40 CME hours, and a Red Sea destination experience. The current site cannot carry that ambition — and AI assistants have almost nothing accurate to cite.

Problem 01

Dated, untrustworthy experience

A generic WordPress and five-builder theme with leftover demo content. A doctor cannot quickly understand the value, trust the brand, or register in a clean flow.

Problem 02

No real conversion path

No persistent Register CTA, no native registration or ticketing, no workshop booking with capacity, and a single catch-all contact form standing in for revenue.

Problem 03

Weak to broken discovery

No real SEO foundation and an AIEO setup that is actively broken — a default llms.txt pointing AI crawlers to a staging domain and a "Hello world!" post.

The recommendation

Rebuild on a custom content management system rather than another WordPress and page-builder stack. A custom CMS, paired with a modern Next.js front end and a cinematic motion layer (GSAP, WebGL and parallax), delivers three things the current stack cannot:

  1. A premium, fast, branded experience that feels like a flagship medical and lifestyle event, not a template.
  2. Bespoke conversion flows built for this event: native registration and ticketing, workshop booking with live capacity limits, and a sponsor and exhibitor pipeline.
  3. Total control over machine-readable output — clean server-rendered HTML, programmatic schema (Event, Course, Person, Organization, FAQ), a real llms.txt, and per-entity metadata generated from the event data.
02 / Current State Audit

Specific findings from the live site.

This is not a styling problem a new theme fixes. It is a structural, content-integrity, conversion and discoverability problem — which is the case for a custom build.

DesignFirst impression

A long single scroll built on a stock festival theme (Festiva). Spacing, typography and rhythm read as a generic template, not a premium medical congress.

  • The hero shows a slogan but does not answer the three five-second questions: what is this, who is it for, what do I do next.
  • The visual language does not signal the dual identity of serious scientific credibility plus carnival and concert energy.
UX & StructureNo clear action

There is no persistent "Register" call to action in a fixed header; the main interaction is a generic form near the bottom of the page.

  • Speakers, workshops, sessions, venue and sponsors live as scroll sections rather than linkable, shareable destinations.
  • Heading structure is broken: no H1 and only four H2 elements.
  • Date inconsistency: the banner reads 13–14 November, the body copy reads 13–15 November.
ContentCritical · integrity

The most urgent issue. The live site still contains leftover template demo content, which is fatal for a premium brand.

  • A section still references "world marketing summit 2024".
  • Slider and block titles render as placeholders: "number-one" through "number-five".
  • Generic marketing blocks remain: "Startup Community", "New Knowledge", "Idea Sharing", and copy about "latest marketing techniques" — none of which belongs on an aesthetic medicine congress.

04 / Redesign Direction

A dual identity: clinical precision, carnival energy.

A refined, editorial, clinical base that opens up into richer, warmer carnival energy in the experience sections — resolving the science-versus-festival tension instead of hiding it.

Visual Language
  • Deep Red Sea night base (navy to deep teal).
  • Warm gold and coral accents for energy.
  • Clean light surfaces for scientific content where readability matters.
  • Confident display headlines, a legible humanist sans for body and data.
  • Real faculty, workshop and venue photography — all stock template imagery retired.
Motion & 3D Layer
  • GSAP + ScrollTrigger scroll storytelling; hero and key numbers (45 speakers, 40 CME, 3 days) count up on view.
  • WebGL / Three.js hero: a subtle Red Sea light environment or particle depth field, lazy-loaded.
  • Parallax depth across venue and experience sections.
  • Interactive agenda and speaker cards with smooth flip, filter and transition.
Performance Guardrails
  • All heavy effects deferred and lazy-loaded.
  • Run off the main thread where possible.
  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Motion never blocks the registration path.

Bilingual from day one — Arabic and English

The audience is primarily Egypt and the wider Arabic-speaking MENA region, yet the current site is English-only and declared en-US. We build the front end and CMS bilingual from the start.

  • Full RTL layout support — correct mirroring of layout, navigation, icons and motion direction, not a mirror hack.
  • Proper Arabic typography and numerals where appropriate.
  • A language switch that persists and is reflected in the URL structure for SEO.
  • hreflang tags and locale-specific metadata generated by the CMS.
  • Localized registration, workshops and trust pages.

06 / Conversion Strategy

Maximize registrations, workshops, and sponsor leads.

The current generic Contact Form 7 cannot do this. The custom CMS powers purpose-built flows around the real conversions: registration, then workshop booking, then sponsor and exhibitor inquiries.

Native registration & ticketing

  • Multi-step registration and checkout modeled in the CMS: attendee type, package, add-ons, details, payment.
  • Group and clinic registrations, early-bird windows, discount codes — managed from the custom admin.
  • Confirmation, QR ticket, calendar add, and email automation.

Workshop booking with capacity

  • Each workshop is a CMS entity with seat capacity, level, trainer, schedule and price.
  • The flow shows live availability ("6 seats left"), prevents overbooking, and supports waitlists.
  • Scarcity is real, and it converts.

Sponsor & exhibitor pipeline

  • A dedicated partner journey with package tiers and an audience and reach summary.
  • Downloadable prospectus and a structured inquiry form routing leads into the admin (and optionally a CRM).

Funnel tightening

  • One primary action above the fold on every page (Register).
  • Remove dead ends — every speaker, session and workshop links to the relevant booking step.
  • Trust at the point of decision: pricing clarity, CME accreditation, refund policy, secure payment badges in checkout.
  • Reduce friction: short forms, autosave on multi-step, mobile-first checkout, clear progress.
  • Measurable micro-conversions: brochure download, newsletter opt-in, and "notify me" for pricing, all tracked.

08 / AIEO Strategy

The competitive edge: be cited by the AI engines.

AIEO means being discoverable and citable inside ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews. The custom CMS controls machine-readable output completely — most competing congresses do none of this.

Programmatic structured data

The CMS emits accurate JSON-LD automatically, kept in sync with content:

  • Event schema for the congress and each session (dates, Place, organizer, offers, eventStatus).
  • Course schema for workshops and CME content.
  • Person schema for every speaker (jobTitle, affiliation, sameAs verified profiles).
  • Organization schema for The Grand Fest as the entity.
  • FAQPage schema on the FAQ and key pages.

A real llms.txt

Replace the broken default file with a CMS-generated llms.txt (and fuller llms-full.txt) summarizing the event, key dates, audience, faculty highlights, workshops, accreditation, and canonical links.

  • It regenerates as content changes, so it never goes stale or points to a wrong domain again.
  • Backed by a single, consistent, machine-readable entity definition across Organization schema, the About page, and llms.txt.

FAQ & comparison content

Structured answers to real questions — "Is the CME recognized in my country?", "Who should attend?", "What is included in each pass?" — in English and Arabic, framed so AI engines can quote them directly.

Machine-readable structure

Semantic HTML, descriptive headings, tables for agenda and pricing, and clear quotable statements rather than slogans — exactly what answer engines extract.

Earn trusted citations

Consistent, correct listings on medical association pages, event aggregators, speaker and sponsor sites, and regional health media that AI tools weight heavily.

11 / Recommended Tech Stack

A custom CMS and modern front end — not WordPress and builders.

A small, owned codebase that gives us total control over performance, conversion flows, and machine-readable output.

Front end

  • Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript
  • Server-rendered, statically optimized, edge-deployed
  • Tailwind CSS design system
  • GSAP + ScrollTrigger
  • Three.js / React Three Fiber hero
  • next-intl for Arabic & English with full RTL

Custom CMS

  • Option A (recommended): Payload CMS (Node/TS) on PostgreSQL — code-defined schema, polished admin, self-hostable.
  • Option B: custom NestJS API + Prisma + PostgreSQL with a Next.js admin.
  • Bilingual fields and per-entity SEO fields.

Conversion

  • Payments: Stripe and/or a local Egyptian gateway (e.g. Paymob).
  • Email automation (transactional + marketing).
  • Programmatic JSON-LD, sitemaps, and llms.txt from CMS data.
  • Optional CRM for sponsor and registration leads.

Why it wins

  • One optimized rendering path vs. five builders loading at once.
  • Small, owned codebase — far lower security and maintenance surface.
  • Full control over HTML, schema and llms.txt.
  • Bespoke flows; multilingual and RTL done right; reusable for 2026+.
