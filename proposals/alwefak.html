01  /  Understanding

A sixty-country exporter. A website that should say so.

Al Wefak Al Saudi has spent seventeen years earning shelf space from Riyadh to Kuala Lumpur. The digital shelf has not kept up. This proposal closes that gap on two fronts at once: the company's front door for buyers and distributors, and a Mero storefront for the Egyptian household.

Established in 2008 in El Obour City as the merger of Egyptian Honey World and Saudi Wadi Al-Nahil Trading & Marketing, Al Wefak Al Saudi runs a 10,000 m² facility producing honey, jams, olive oil, herbal tea, tomato paste, ketchup, mustard, mayonnaise, chocolate spread, date syrup and sugarcane molasses. It pioneered one-dose glass jars for hotels and airlines, holds Halal, US FDA, SFDA and Kosher certification, and exports roughly 80% of output to more than sixty countries.

Two requests came to us. First, a static corporate website that presents the company, its categories, its certifications and its private-label capability to buyers, distributors and HORECA purchasers in English and Arabic. Second, a Shopify store that sells Mero directly to consumers in Egypt — where Euromonitor named the brand the most dynamic sweet-spreads company of 2025, with e-commerce as its fastest-growing channel.

Two audiences

A Carrefour buyer in Jeddah and a mother in Nasr City want different things. One site each, one brand system across both.

Reference-grade

Heinz sets the bar for a food brand's corporate presence. Organimo sets it for scroll-driven product storytelling. We match both, in your colours.

Arabic where it sells

The store is Arabic-first with English available. The corporate site is English-first with full Arabic RTL. Neither is a translation bolt-on.

Owned

Code, Shopify account, domains, product data and content belong to Al Wefak Al Saudi. NixAI builds and hands over.

01.1  /  The current site

What alwefakalsaudi.com does today.

We reviewed the live site while preparing this document. These are the things a buyer or a shopper will notice before they notice the honey.

It was down when we checked

On 16 September the homepage returned HTTP 500 for several minutes. A buyer landing from Gulfood or a search result sees a server error, not a brand.

Placeholder product data

Product pages list pallet configuration and unit size as "According to the packaging". For a buyer, that is the one table that matters.

No way to buy

Mero is on Egyptian supermarket shelves and marketplaces, yet the company's own domain cannot take an order, a lead, or an email address.

Template, not brand

A generic catalogue theme with stock section layouts. None of the catalogue's honeycomb system, sand accents or product photography direction carries over.

Duplicate indexing

Google returns the same pages under www., bare domain and mail.alwefakalsaudi.com. Search authority is split three ways.

A person's inbox in the header

An individual employee's email is the site-wide contact. Distributor enquiries deserve a routed form with an owner, a response time and a record.

06  /  Architecture

Static where it should be static. Shopify where it should sell.

The corporate site has no database and no server to fall over — it is pre-rendered and served from the edge. The store runs on Shopify's hosted platform, so checkout, payments and uptime are their problem, not yours.

Corporate site — Next.js static export

  • Pre-rendered HTML and assets on Vercel or Cloudflare Pages, global CDN, HTTPS
  • Content in structured files (products, categories, certificates, news) editable by your team in a Git-backed editor
  • English and Arabic routes with full RTL layout, hreflang and canonical tags
  • Forms routed via a serverless function to email, Google Sheets or your CRM
  • GSAP ScrollTrigger for pinned chapters, reveals and parallax; motion respects reduced-motion settings

Mero store — Shopify Online Store 2.0

  • Custom Liquid theme with JSON templates; every section configurable in the Shopify editor
  • Arabic-first with English via Translate & Adapt; EGP pricing; Shopify Markets ready for KSA and Gulf
  • Payments: Paymob or Fawry gateway (cards, wallets, Fawry codes), cash on delivery, ValU instalments
  • Shipping profiles by governorate with Bosta or Mylerz integration; WhatsApp notifications
  • Apps kept minimal: Klaviyo, a reviews app, a bundles app — each chosen for Arabic support

Shared product data

  • One master SKU sheet: names in both languages, category, sizes, nutrition, shelf life, pallet data, certifications, imagery
  • Published to the corporate catalogue as static pages and to Shopify as products and metafields
  • Structured data (Product, Recipe, Organization) on both surfaces for search and AI engines
  • Cross-links: a spec sheet on the corporate site points to the retail size on the store, and back

Domains, ownership & care

  • alwefakalsaudi.com for the corporate site; mero.eg (or shop.alwefakalsaudi.com) for the store — your choice, your registrar
  • Shopify account, hosting accounts, repositories and analytics all opened in Al Wefak Al Saudi's name
  • 301 redirects from every current URL; www, bare and mail hostnames consolidated
  • Documentation, recorded training, and 30 days of hypercare after each launch
