01 / Executive Summary

The website has ingredients. It needs a system.

Connect Financials has MT5 access, a $100 entry point, Islamic account support, Arabic and English channels, fast execution claims, and SFSA regulation. The current website does not yet package those assets into a premium financial experience.

Problem 01

Outdated perception

The site needs to feel like a modern financial platform, not a broker template with feature blocks.

Problem 02

Weak system story

The current site lists spreads, MT5, accounts, and assets. It does not show the trading journey or why a trader should choose Connect Financials.

Problem 03

Low SEO and AI visibility

The site needs stronger search foundations and a clear AIEO layer so AI assistants can identify, understand, and cite the brand correctly.

02 / Current State Audit

Specific findings from the live site.

The audit reviewed the homepage, About, Trading Accounts, Tradable Assets, Payment Methods, and Partners pages, plus rendered desktop and mobile behavior.

Visual DesignPremium gap

The homepage uses a dark space-like hero and the line "ConnectTradeAchieve," which points in the right direction. The execution is still mixed with generic cards, repeated buttons, inconsistent spacing, and no meaningful product interface story.

  • The hero says "Experience the future of forex trading with ultra-low spreads, lightning execution, and low commissions." This is clear, but generic.
  • The live market card shows "US30 0.00000" while XAU/USD and USOIL show live-looking prices, weakening the real-time data promise.
  • The large risk-warning overlay blocks the conversion area on desktop and dominates the mobile first impression.
UX and FunnelPortal friction

The marketing site pushes users to `portal.connectfinancials.com`, but the conversion path is not clean enough.

  • "Trade Now," "Try Free Demo," "Open Account," and "Sign Up" often point to the same sign-in route.
  • A new visitor clicking "Sign Up" is sent to `portal.connectfinancials.com/auth/jwt/sign-in`, which reads like a login screen rather than registration.
  • The homepage says "No minimum deposit" near the bottom, while account pages state a $100 minimum deposit.
  • The site does not clearly separate "Open Live Account," "Open Demo Account," and "Login."
MessagingGeneric claims

The current copy says what Connect Financials offers, but not why the offer matters. It uses category-common language that competitors can also claim.

  • The homepage states "TRUSTED BY 50,000+ TRADERS," the About page says "3,000+ Active Traders," and the Trading Accounts page says "100,000+ Satisfied clients worldwide."
  • The Trading Accounts page says "Compare our four distinct account types," then shows three accounts.
  • The Raw account is described as "Commission-based model" on one page, while other sections say "No commission fees."
TrustCompliance risk

Trust can be strengthened, but only through accurate, supportable statements. The site currently has conflicting regulatory references and some risky claims.

  • The homepage includes "FSCS Protected." FSCS is a UK compensation scheme and should not appear unless clients are actually covered.
  • The About page references Seychelles and license SD244, while Trading Accounts references Comoros CFSA, license HY00523008, and a Comoros address.
  • Claims such as "world's most trusted forex broker" and "client funds protected" need substantiation or replacement.
MobileCritical issue

At a 390px mobile viewport, the homepage rendered as a narrow left column with a large blank area to the right. The risk warning covered the hero and primary CTA.

  • The first visible experience is compliance friction, not product value.
  • The chat widget and risk banner compete for limited mobile space.
  • The live market card starts below the overlay and does not create a premium mobile impression.
SEO and AIEOMissing foundation

The site has basic pages but weak search and AI extraction signals.

  • Homepage title is simply "Connect Financials."
  • The meta description is "Your Gateway to Seamless Trading," which is too vague.
  • No canonical tag or JSON-LD schema was detected on the homepage.
  • `sitemap.xml` returns 404 and `llms.txt` returns 404.
  • The site lacks AI-friendly answers such as "Is Connect Financials regulated?" and "Does Connect Financials offer Islamic accounts?"
TechnicalPerformance budget

The homepage loaded with 49 observed resources during browser inspection, including 23 scripts, 8 CSS/link resources, 4 video resources, and multiple fetch/XHR calls. The inspected load event completed in roughly 3.4 seconds.

  • Live market data appears partly broken or inconsistent.
  • Visual effects should be performance-budgeted before adding GSAP and Three.js.
  • Mobile Core Web Vitals should be treated as a design requirement.

04 / Redesign Direction

Dark, electric, premium and product-led.

The redesign should feel closer to a fintech trading terminal than a generic broker template. Motion should reveal the trading journey, not decorate the page.

Visual Language
  • Deep navy and near-black foundation.
  • Electric blue, cyan, and controlled lime accents.
  • Glassmorphism for market cards, account selectors, and execution stats.
  • High-contrast typography with compact trading data.
GSAP Motion
  • Hero sequence with market grid, data points, MT5 interface and CTA reveal.
  • Account selector animations for Standard, Raw, Demo and Islamic paths.
  • Execution story from spread to order to confirmation.
  • Trust layer proof cards with controlled reveals.
Three.js
  • Lightweight 3D market globe or liquidity field in the hero.
  • Premium asset universe section for forex, gold, oil, indices and crypto.
  • Static or CSS fallback visuals on mobile.

How the new design sells the system

  1. Understand the promise: trade global markets with MT5, fast execution, and MENA-ready support.
  2. See the platform: real MT5 screens, mobile flow, live spreads, tools, and execution highlights.
  3. Choose the account: simple account comparison with honest spread and commission rules.
  4. Explore assets: forex, gold, oil, indices, and crypto with market conditions and example use cases.
  5. Fund confidently: cards, bank transfer, USDT, and regional payment options explained clearly.
  6. Verify trust: legal entity, SFSA license SD244, risk warning, restrictions, support, and policies.
  7. Start safely: demo first, then live account when ready.

Arabic and RTL

  • Full RTL layout support with mirrored navigation, cards, icons, and motion direction.
  • Arabic typography selected for financial readability.
  • Arabic FAQs and education content written natively for MENA traders.
  • Separate Arabic metadata, schema, and hreflang implementation.

Trading Terminology

Avoid literal translation where regional traders expect terms such as MT5, spread, swap-free, leverage, margin and CFD. The Arabic experience should be localized, not simply translated.

06 / Conversion Strategy

Tighten the funnel from marketing site to portal.

The site should move visitors into the right portal action with more confidence and less ambiguity.

Funnel Fixes

  • Separate "Open Live Account," "Open Demo Account," and "Login" into distinct CTAs.
  • Send sign-up clicks to a registration route, not a sign-in route, if the portal supports it.
  • Add a pre-portal transition page or modal explaining what happens next.
  • Add persistent but non-intrusive CTAs on mobile.
  • Use a compact risk disclosure pattern with expandable full details.
  • Add account recommendation logic for new traders, low-spread traders, demo users, and swap-free users.

Homepage Conversion Blocks

  • Hero with premium market visual and two CTAs.
  • Live spread strip with timestamp and source.
  • MT5 experience with desktop and mobile screens.
  • Account chooser for Standard, Raw, Demo and Islamic.
  • MENA funding section for cards, bank transfer, USDT and Whish where applicable.
  • Trust section with SFSA SD244 and risk context.

  • Implement hreflang for English and Arabic.
  • Add breadcrumbs and structured data.
  • Optimize Core Web Vitals, especially mobile LCP and CLS.

On-Page SEO

  • Improve titles and meta descriptions across all pages.
  • Add FAQs to each commercial page.
  • Create specific trading-condition pages.
  • Add glossary content for MENA traders.
  • Add localized Arabic content around Islamic accounts, gold trading, forex trading and USDT deposits.

Authority Building

  • Earn citations through Arabic finance publications, webinars, and partner education.
  • Encourage reviews only through compliant, authentic processes.

08 / AIEO Strategy

Make Connect Financials discoverable and citable in AI answers.

AIEO makes the brand easier for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews to understand, compare, and cite.

Clean Entity Definition

  • Brand: Connect Financials.
  • Legal entity: Connect Financials Ltd.
  • Website: `connectfinancials.com`.
  • Portal: `portal.connectfinancials.com`.
  • Regulator: Seychelles Financial Services Authority.
  • License: SD244.
  • Platform: MetaTrader 5.
  • Products: forex CFDs, commodities CFDs, indices CFDs, crypto CFDs.
  • Languages: English and Arabic.

Structured Data

  • Organization schema.
  • FinancialService schema.
  • WebSite schema.
  • BreadcrumbList schema.
  • FAQPage schema.
  • Article schema for education content.
  • Service schema for account types where compliant.
  • SameAs links for verified social channels and profiles.

`llms.txt`

Create `https://connectfinancials.com/llms.txt` with verified legal facts, product summary, platform summary, risk disclosure, canonical links, contact paths, and citation guidance for AI systems.

FAQ Answer Blocks

Answer real questions such as "Is Connect Financials regulated?", "What platform does it use?", "Does it offer Islamic accounts?", "What is the minimum deposit?", and "How does it compare with Exness, XM, FXTM and HFM?"

Citable Sources

Clean up public profiles, pursue citations in broker directories and Arabic finance media, publish named market commentary, and add a public regulation page AI tools can cite safely.

09 / Trust and Compliance

Handle the regulation question honestly.

Connect Financials should not hide offshore regulation, and it should not overstate it. The better strategy is clarity, consistency, and responsible risk framing.

Immediate Cleanup

  • Remove or verify "FSCS Protected" immediately.
  • Standardize regulatory references to SFSA license SD244 if that is the correct current status.
  • Remove conflicting Comoros license and address references unless a separate active entity must be disclosed.
  • Replace "Trusted & Regulated Worldwide" with precise wording such as "Licensed by the SFSA, License SD244."

Trust Layer

  • Add a dedicated Regulation and Legal page.
  • Add a "How client funds are handled" page with precise statements.
  • Use a clear risk disclosure above or near CTAs without blocking the entire experience.
  • Add verified support hours, phone number formatting, office addresses, complaints, AML, KYC and refund policy links.
