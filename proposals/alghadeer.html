01  /  Where you stand

A developer of cities, with two brochures.

We audited both domains the way a buyer in Baghdad, a broker in Najaf and an investor in London would. The brand is strong. The projects are real and enormous. The digital layer is two disconnected pamphlets that do not agree with each other, and neither of them can take a lead past a WhatsApp link.

alghadeerrealestate.com · English

A Framer template, published as-is.

FramerEnglish onlyLenis scrollgtag
  • Related-project cards render undefined undefined where the unit count and completion should be.
  • Partner categories are machine-translated: The perpetrators and Donors stand where “Contractors” and “Financiers” should.
  • Project pages show a single headline number — the total project value — and a “contact for details” button. No unit types, prices, plans, availability or payment terms.
  • No Arabic. The primary market reads the secondary site.
  • The only conversion path is a WhatsApp deep link and a contact form with no destination system behind it.
alghadeer.com · Arabic

A hand-built SPA, better looking, equally alone.

Vite + ReactTailwindArabic onlyNo analytics
  • The “from plan to reality” counter and the four headline statistics sit at 0 for visitors — the numbers never animate in.
  • The EN toggle leads to a different company story on a different domain, so search engines index two competing brands.
  • Project pages list three unit sizes and one enquiry form. No pricing, no phase status, no gallery captions, no brochure, no map.
  • Client-rendered: crawlers see an empty shell, so the site that speaks the market’s language is the one Google ranks least.
  • No CRM, no pixel, no retargeting, no attribution. A million YouTube subscribers arrive and leave anonymous.

Two websites, two different truths.

The same project is described with different facts depending on the language a buyer reads. For a developer selling trust in advance of delivery, this is the most expensive bug on either site.

    06  /  Architecture

    Built for Iraq. Owned by Al Ghadeer.

    One API, one identity, one inventory. Website, app, CRM and automation are clients of the same platform, so a change to a unit price is made once and is true everywhere within a second.

    Platform & data

    • Node.js services, PostgreSQL for inventory and CRM, MongoDB for content and events
    • Projects → phases → blocks → units, with statuses, price lists and holds
    • Single customer record shared by website, app, CRM and automation
    • Event bus: “unit reserved”, “instalment paid”, “lead scored” drive every downstream action

    Surfaces

    • Next.js website — server-rendered Arabic and English, RTL by design
    • React Native app — iOS and Android from one codebase
    • Payload CMS — bilingual editing, roles, approval workflow
    • NixAI CRM and automation console in Arabic and English for the sales floor

    Iraq specifics

    • Payments: Qi Card, ZainCash, FIB and AsiaHawala for Iraq; international cards for the diaspora
    • WhatsApp Business API as the primary channel; SMS via Zain, Asiacell and Korek aggregators
    • Hosting in-region (AWS Bahrain or UAE) or on your own servers in Baghdad — your choice, documented
    • Arabic-first content model with Kurdish and English as first-class locales

    Why this should be NixAI.

    You are not buying a website. You are buying the operating system of a developer that intends to be compared with Emaar — and a partner who will still be here when Al-Ridha's next company needs the same platform.

    Five pillars, one team

    Website, CMS, app, CRM and automation are built by one team against one platform. There is no integration project afterwards, because there was never a seam.

    Arabic-first, not translated

    Arabic copy, RTL layouts, Iraqi phone formats, local payment rails and WhatsApp as the primary channel are in the design from day one — not bolted onto an English template.

    Client provides

    Brand assets, project data and renders, unit lists and price sheets, payment-gateway merchant accounts, Apple and Google developer accounts (or we open them in your name), WhatsApp Business verification, and timely feedback from a named product owner.

    Not included

    Store fees, gateway and telecom fees, WhatsApp conversation charges, paid media budgets, third-party licences (maps, video hosting, 360° capture), photography, drone and 3D-modelling production, and hosting after the first 12 months.

    Revisions

    Each wave includes two structured review rounds. Further design or scope change is estimated before work continues.

    Intellectual property

    Upon final payment, all custom code and design for Al Ghadeer assign to the client. Open-source components remain under their own licences.

    Group reuse

    The platform is multi-brand by design. Extending it to another Al-Ridha Investment Group company is a configuration and design exercise, quoted separately at a fraction of this programme.
