02 — Scope of Work

What We Deliver

Every component is handled end-to-end by the NixAI team — one accountable partner from kickoff to launch and beyond.

  • Domain Acquisition — Register the ideal .com domain for AM Jewelry, configure DNS records, and activate SSL/HTTPS security.
  • Shopify Store Setup — Initialize Shopify, install a luxury theme, configure collections, product pages, size guides, and navigation.
  • Design Customization — Brand-aligned colors, typography, logo placement, hero sections, and homepage layout to reflect AM Jewelry's aesthetic.
  • Payment & Checkout — Stripe, PayPal, and/or local payment provider integration with a streamlined order confirmation flow.
  • SEO & Analytics — On-page SEO foundation, Google Analytics 4, Meta Pixel integration, and sitemap submission.
  • AI Try-On Plugin — Development — Custom Shopify embedded app: customer uploads a photo, AI generates a realistic image of them wearing the selected jewelry.
  • AI Try-On Plugin — Integration — "Try It On" button embedded on product pages, mobile-responsive interface, download and share functionality.
  • Monthly Maintenance & Support — Product updates, content changes, performance monitoring, security patches, and priority support channel (up to 10 hrs/month).
